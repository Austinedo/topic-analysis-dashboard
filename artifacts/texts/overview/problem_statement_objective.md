### < Problem Statement >

> Local businesses increasingly depend on their online reputation to attract new customers and retain existing ones. Online review platforms such as Google Reviews and Yelp provide a rich and accessible source of authentic customer feedback. However, independently owned businesses often lack the analytical tools needed to extract useful operational insights from this unstructured text.
>
> Over time, a business may accumulate hundreds or thousands of reviews that discuss a broad range of experiences, including service quality, cleanliness, wait times, pricing, staff interactions, and product quality. Without a structured analytical framework, recurring patterns within this feedback remain buried in individual reviews. This limits a business owner's ability to make informed, data-driven decisions that improve operations and customer satisfaction.
>
> Customer-feedback analysis is often performed manually or reduced to aggregate star ratings. Although star ratings summarize overall sentiment, they provide limited information about the specific operational issues underlying positive or negative customer experiences. Consequently, businesses may struggle to identify recurring problems and prioritize areas for improvement.


### < Objective >

> The objective of this anaylsis is to apply natural language processing (NLP) and classical machine-learning techniques to Google Reviews collected from a randomly selected, anonymous local business. The analysis focuses on negative customer reviews and uses unsupervised topic-discovery methods to identify recurring operational themes.
>
> The resulting themes are intended to help business owners better understand the issues that drive negative customer experiences and prioritize practical areas for operational improvement.
